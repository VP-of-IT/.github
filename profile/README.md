# VP of IT – IT Consulting

B2B IT consulting: fractional CTO, embedded tech lead and full-stack delivery for startups, SMBs and software agencies.

Founded by [Dušan Boškić](https://github.com/DBoskic), former CTO and VP IT at DistantJob, with almost three decades in software delivery.

## Engagement models

- **Fractional CTO** — monthly retainer covering technology roadmap, architecture and vendor decisions, and infrastructure and DevOps direction.
- **Embedded tech lead** — hands-on leadership inside your team: architecture, code review and delivery ownership.
- **Project delivery** — fixed-scope builds of full-stack web applications, APIs and integrations, and Chrome extensions (Manifest V3).
- **Agency partnership** — subcontracted or white-label senior development capacity for agencies and consultancies.
- **Project recovery** — assessment and stabilisation of stalled or failing software projects.

## Stack

TypeScript, React, Node.js, Bun, PHP (LAMP), MERN, Electron, Chrome extensions (MV3), MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, self-hosted Linux.

## Working terms

Direct B2B contract and invoicing with a registered Serbian company. Remote-first, English, overlap with EU and US East Coast hours, based in Belgrade.
